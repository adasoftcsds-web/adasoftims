Barangay Services

Residency Certificate›

A certificate issued by the barangay to prove that the applicant is a resident of the community.

Barangay Clearance›

A document certifying that the resident has no pending case or complaint in the barangay.

Barangay ID›

An official identification card issued by the barangay for its constituents.

Certificate of Indigency (Free)›

A certificate proving that the resident belongs to a low-income household.

Business Clearance (Municipality-dependent)›

A certification required for registering and operating a business, usually processed at the municipality.

Other Certificates (Depends on request)›

Solo Parent Certificate — A certificate recognizing the applicant as a solo parent.

Good Moral Character — A certificate attesting that the resident is in good standing within the community.

First-Time Job Seeker Certificate (Free) — A certificate for first-time job applicants to avail of government-mandated free clearances.

Free Services: Indigency Certificate, First-Time Job Seeker Certificate.

With Fees: Residency, Barangay Clearance, Barangay ID, Business Clearance, Solo Parent, Good Moral Character.

Processing Time: Usually same day, unless verification requires additional checks.

Applicability: All services are available to barangay constituents.

Katarungang Pambarangay Services

1Filing of Complaint

Complainant fills out KP Form 7 (Complaint Form). Presents a valid ID and details of the grievance. Pays the filing fee if applicable (depends on barangay policy). The Barangay Secretary records the complaint and schedules mediation.

2Jurisdiction Check

The Lupon determines if the case is within barangay authority. If covered, it proceeds to mediation. If not covered, the complainant is advised to file with the proper authority.

3Mediation

The Punong Barangay meets with the complainant and respondent. Both parties present their side. The Punong Barangay offers advice, recommendations, or possible solutions. If an agreement is reached, the settlement is recorded in a KP Form, signed by both parties, and copies are issued. If no agreement is reached, the case proceeds to conciliation.

4Conciliation (Pangkat ng Tagapagkasundo)

The case is referred to a panel of three conciliators. Hearings are conducted with both parties present. The Pangkat facilitates negotiation and compromise. If an agreement is reached, the settlement is recorded, signed by all parties, and copies are issued. If no agreement is reached, a Certificate to File Action (CFA) is issued.

5Settlement Agreement

A written agreement binding both parties. It is recorded in KP Forms and signed by the complainant, respondent, and Lupon/Pangkat. Copies are given to both parties, and the barangay monitors compliance.

6Certificate to File Action (CFA)

Issued when no settlement is reached or the respondent refuses to participate. This allows the complainant to elevate the case to court. Barangay records are forwarded to the proper authority.

7Resolution

The case ends either through settlement or CFA issuance. The barangay closes the records and monitors compliance if a settlement was made.

Fees: Filing may or may not have fees depending on barangay policy.

Free Stages: Mediation, conciliation, settlement, CFA issuance, and resolution.

Mandatory: Most disputes must pass through barangay conciliation before reaching the courts.

Binding: Signed settlements have the force of law and are enforceable.
